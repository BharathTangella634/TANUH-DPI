How It Works

Three conversion steps running securely on the clinical platform

01

Audio Preprocessing

Audio files are normalised, noise-filtered, and segmented to prepare them for optimal transcription accuracy.

02

Speech-to-Text

Our specialised Indic-Conformer model processes the audio to generate a highly accurate, time-stamped raw transcript.

03

Clinical NLP Summarisation

Medical NLP models analyze the raw transcript to generate structured summaries, extracting key diagnoses and treatment plans.

Plausible 100% Confidential On-Premises Execution

Deploy the Medical Audio Summarisation service locally with a simple CPU-based installation. No external API calls, no cloud storage, and all processing occurs securely in memory. Designed to support compliance with the Indian DPDP Act for privacy-preserving healthcare applications.

Service Limitations

Audio Format & Size Limits

Maximum audio file upload is capped at 25 MB. The audio must be a minimum of 5 mins long, and it should be a .wav file sampled at 16kHz.

Processing Time

Processing long audio recordings may take up to 7-10 mins due to the deep learning models running securely on local CPUs.

Audio Quality Dependency

Transcription accuracy is highly dependent on audio quality, background noise, and microphone clarity. Always review the generated summary.
