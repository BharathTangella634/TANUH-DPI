Service Limitations

Input Pairing Required

The Non-Contrast Head CT scan and report must belong to the same study. Mismatched studies can lead to unreliable verification tags.

Image Quality Dependency

Motion artifacts, incomplete volumes, missing slices, or low-quality scans may reduce finding-level confidence.

Clinical Review Required

Verification tags are intended to assist quality review and should not replace radiologist judgment or clinical decision-making.
